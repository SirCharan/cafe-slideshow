## Production & Speech Synthesis Guidelines
1. **Pacing:** Strict adherence to pauses. The 1.5s - 2.5s pauses between lines provide essential space for animated financial charts, line graphics, and UI mockups to breathe.
2. **Audio Levels:** 
   - Dramatic Voice (`ref_expressive_1`): -16 LUFS, rich low-end presence, close-mic proximity.
   - Financial Math Voice (`ref4`): -14 LUFS, crisp, clinical, broadcast punch.
3. **Phonetic Pronunciations:** Acronyms like `HSR`, `BHK`, `UPI`, `BESCOM`, `ACT`, `BBMP`, `RWA` must be vocalized letter-by-letter (`H-S-R`, `B-H-K`, `U-P-I`). Number denominations (`lakhs`, `crores`, `thousands`) must be rendered verbatim as expanded text in TTS manifests.
