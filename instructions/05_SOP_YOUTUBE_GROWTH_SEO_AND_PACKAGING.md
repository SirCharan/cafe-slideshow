# 📈 SOP 05: YouTube Growth, SEO & Packaging Architecture
**Standard**: High-CTR A/B Packaging & Algorithmic Retention Engineering  
**Scope**: Titles, Descriptions, Chapters, 25 Canonical Tags, Pinned Comments, Endscreens  

---

## 1. Title Formulation Engine

Every video release must generate 3 distinct title variants:
- **Variant A (The Conversational Challenge - Primary)**:
  `So You Want to Own a [Business]? [Punchy Sub-Hook]` (< 75 characters)
  *Example*: `So You Want to Own a Bangalore Café? The Brutal P&L Behind Indie Coffee`
- **Variant B (The Financial Contrast)**:
  `The [Big Revenue/Capex] Trap | Why [X]% Shut Down in [Y] Months`
  *Example*: `The ₹1.5 Crore Bangalore Café Hype Trap | Why 80% Shut Down in 18 Months`
- **Variant C (The Contrarian Math)**:
  `[Everyday Micro Cost] vs. [Massive Fixed Cost] (Forensic Unit Economics)`
  *Example*: `How a ₹220 Cold Brew Bleeds ₹4.5L/Month in Indiranagar (Forensic Unit Economics)`

---

## 2. Description Architecture (Narrative + Forensic Value)

1. **The Hook (Above the fold, 2 lines)**: Immediate contrarian question.
2. **The Forensic P&L Summary**: Clean, formatted bullet list of the actual monthly balance sheet (rent, power, payroll, spoilage, net margin). This drives high algorithmic search authority.
3. **Clickable Chapter Timestamps**: Minimum 7 to 10 distinct timestamped sections.
4. **Curated Next Video Links**: Direct links to keep viewers inside our ecosystem playlist.
5. **Hashtags**: 5 to 7 high-intent niche hashtags (`#BangaloreCafe`, `#UnitEconomics`, `#StartupIndia`).

---

## 3. Pinned Discussion Comment Protocol

Never post a generic *"What did you think?"* comment. Every pinned comment must:
1. Provoke a polarized debate between two passionate groups (e.g., café owners vs laptop workers, tenants vs PG landlords).
2. Ask an actionable, concrete scenario question.
3. Acknowledge the relatable absurdity of Bangalore tech culture.

---

## 4. Endscreens & Cards
- **Timing**: Starts at `Video Duration - 20s`.
- **Card 1**: Subscribe Pill (Center-Right).
- **Card 2**: Best for Viewer or Dedicated Series Playlist (Center-Left).
