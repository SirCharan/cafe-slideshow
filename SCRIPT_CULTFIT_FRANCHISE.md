## Act 2: The ₹2.2 Crore Sunk Cost (1:15 - 3:45)
- **Scene 4 (The Upfront Fitout)**:  
  "But before a single member laces up their sneakers, your bank account takes a massive hit."  
  *[500ms pause]*  
  "First: a ₹20 Lakh non-refundable franchise fee. Then: the physical fitout. Cult requires double-layered acoustic rubber flooring, soundproofed walls, high-lumen neon rig lighting, and premium shower locker rooms. That is ₹85 Lakhs."  
- **Scene 5 (The Equipment Capex)**:  
  "Next comes the gear: concept-2 rowers, assault air bikes, custom pull-up rigs, and calibrated urethane dumbbells. Another ₹55 Lakhs."  
  *[400ms pause]*  
  "Factor in pre-operative marketing, security deposits, and working capital... and you have invested ₹2.2 Crores before day one."  
- **Scene 6 (The High Street Rent Trap)**:  
  "To generate Cult-level footfalls, you cannot open in a quiet basement. You need a 4,500 square-foot floor plate on a high-street in Indiranagar, HSR Layout, or Whitefield."  
  *[450ms pause]*  
  "At ₹90 per square foot, your monthly rent is ₹4,05,000. And unlike gym members who can pause their memberships... your landlord expects his cheque on the first of every month."

## Act 3: The Operational Meat Grinder (3:45 - 6:30)
- **Scene 7 (The Electricity Shock)**:  
  "Cooling 4,500 square feet packed with forty sweaty athletes during an HRX session requires a 50-ton commercial HVAC system running sixteen hours a day."  
  *[400ms pause]*  
  "On Karnataka's non-domestic commercial tariff, your monthly power bill routinely crosses ₹1,40,000."  
- **Scene 8 (The Trainer Talent War)**:  
  "Cult's secret weapon has always been its charismatic trainers. But star trainers don't come cheap."  
  *[500ms pause]*  
  "Four certified Cult trainers and two support coaches will run you ₹2,50,000 a month. And if your lead trainer quits to start private coaching, twenty of your members walk out the door with them."  
- **Scene 9 (The Brand Squeeze)**:  
  "Then comes the platform tax. Cult.fit charges a 12% royalty on total collections for managing the app, marketing, and workout programming."  
  *[450ms pause]*  
  "Plus, when Cult runs aggressive promotional sales on Cult Pass Elite, your per-session payout shrinks... but your operating costs remain locked in stone."

## Act 4: The 60% Churn Trap & The True Verdict (6:30 - 9:00)
- **Scene 10 (The Annual Churn Collapse)**:  
  "Here is the dirty secret of Indian fitness: in January, your center is overflowing. By April, sixty percent of your members have stopped showing up."  
  *[500ms pause]*  
  "In a traditional gym, absentee members are pure profit. But in Cult's class-based model, if footfalls drop, your app utilization falls, and Curefit's algorithm begins deprioritizing your center."  
- **Scene 11 (The True Net Profit)**:  
  "When you subtract rent, electricity, trainer payroll, equipment maintenance, and Curefit royalties from your eight lakhs in revenue... your true monthly net profit is just ₹1,35,000."  
  *[600ms pause]*  
  "That is a 16% net margin for managing broken assault bikes, flooded shower rooms, and non-stop member complaints."  
- **Scene 12 (The Final Payback Verdict)**:  
  "At ₹1.35 Lakhs a month, it will take you fifty-four months—four and a half years—just to recover your initial capital. If you had simply parked that ₹2.2 Crores in senior debt or mutual funds, you would have made more money while sleeping through the night."  
  *[400ms pause]*  
  "Cult.fit built a world-class consumer brand. But as a franchisee... you aren't disrupting fitness. You are financing Curefit's real estate footprint with your own capital."
