## Act 2: The Sunk Capital Trap (1:20 - 2:40)

### Scene 3: The ₹1.5 Crore Vanity Fitout
- **Visual**: 2.5D comic cutaway of the cafe interior. Price tags aggressively slam down onto fixtures with a heavy metal thud: `₹12,00,000` onto a La Marzocco espresso machine, `₹8,00,000` onto Italian acoustic ceiling panels, `₹15,00,000` onto polished microcement flooring.  
- **Voiceover**:  
  "Before you pour a single espresso, your bank account enters the ICU."  
  *[500ms pause]*  
  "Specialty coffee in Bangalore is a design arms race. You need a twelve-lakh La Marzocco machine, custom terrazzo counters, and curated Scandinavian chairs. That is one point two crores of sunk capital before opening day."  

### Scene 4: The 100-Foot Road Master Lease
- **Visual**: A towering cartoon landlord in a safari suit holding a giant padlocked lease deed, while counting thick stacks of currency notes.  
- **Voiceover**:

## Act 3: The Operational Meat Grinder (2:40 - 4:10)

### Scene 5: The Laptop Squatter Paradox
- **Visual**: Comic scene of the cafe dining area. Two customers sit at a 4-person wooden table surrounded by laptops, iPad stands, phone chargers, and extension cords. One empty coffee cup with a single melting ice cube sits between them. A digital clock on the wall rapidly spins forward from 11:00 AM to 5:00 PM.  
- **Voiceover**:  
  "In a regular restaurant, a table turns four times an afternoon. In a Bangalore specialty café... it turns zero point eight times."  
  *[500ms pause]*  
  "Because two senior engineers will buy one two-hundred-rupee cold brew, plug in two MacBooks, use your gigabit Wi-Fi, and conduct five Zoom sprints over six hours."  

### Scene 6: The Physical Conveyor Belt of Spoilage
- **Visual**: An animated conveyor belt carrying freshly baked almond croissants, sourdough sandwiches, and cartons of artisanal oat milk. The belt rolls directly off the counter and dumps the food into a large green dumpster labeled `100% LOSS (24-HR EXPIRY)`.  
- **Voiceover**:  
  "Coffee beans last months. But artisan pastries and fresh milk do not."  
  *[450ms pause]*  
  "If you bake thirty sourdough sandwiches and sell twelve, the remaining eighteen go straight into the dumpster at 10:00 PM. That is an invisible thirty-thousand-rupee bleed every single month."  

### Scene 7: The BESCOM & Genset Burn
- **Visual**: A massive mechanical gear system with smoke billowing out of a commercial diesel generator behind the café. A giant digital electricity meter spins wildly like a casino slot machine.  
- **Voiceover**:  
  "Cooling a fifty-seater glass greenhouse under the April Bangalore sun requires eight split air conditioners running continuously."  
  *[400ms pause]*  
  "Between Karnataka's commercial electricity tariff and sixty liters of diesel for your backup generator... power alone eats one lakh eighty thousand rupees a month."  

### Scene 8: The Barista Talent War
- **Visual**: A head barista wearing a leather apron with an intricate mustache holding a milk pitcher. A competing café hand reaches in from off-screen waving a bundle of 500-rupee notes and plucks him away.  
- **Voiceover**:  
  "A great café lives and dies by its head barista. But in Bangalore, specialty baristas are scouted like IPL cricketers."  
  *[450ms pause]*  
  "The moment your barista masters swan latte art, the venture-funded chain across the street offers him a thirty percent raise, leaving you with burnt beans and untrained apprentices."

## Act 4: The True Balance Sheet & The Verdict (4:10 - 5:30)

### Scene 9: The Whiteboard Cash-Flow Grinder
- **Visual**: A large mechanical balance sheet whiteboard. 100 customer bank notes enter on the left. Massive metal grinding gears crush them: 45% drops into a box labeled `LANDLORD`, 20% into `BESCOM & DIESEL`, 18% into `STAFF PAYROLL`, 10% into `SPOILED FOOD & BEANS`.  
- **Voiceover**:  
  "When you feed your daily customer revenue through the actual operational machine, the math completely collapses."  
  *[500ms pause]*  
  "Your four lakh fifty thousand rent, power, payroll, and spoilage chew up ninety percent of your incoming cash before you ever touch a single rupee."  

### Scene 10: The True Monthly P&L
- **Visual**: A clean forensic P&L summary graphic appears on a clipboard held by the Anurag Mascot. The bottom line flashes in subtle crimson and amber.  
- **Voiceover**:  
  "At four hundred cups a day with food, an Indiranagar café generates ten lakh rupees in gross sales."  
  *[400ms pause]*  
  "After deducting fixed rent, utilities, raw materials, GST, and payroll... the operator walks away with forty thousand rupees. In a bad monsoon month... they lose eighty-five thousand."  

### Scene 11: The Secret Real Winners
- **Visual**: Comic split screen. Left side: The stressed café owner sweeping floors at midnight. Right side: A wealthy B2B coffee bean roaster in a tailored suit drinking an espresso in front of a shipping warehouse with stacks of 50kg burlap sacks.  
- **Voiceover**:  
  "So who is actually making money in India's coffee boom?"  
  *[600ms pause]*  
  "Not the café owner. The real winners are the commercial property owners... and the B2B specialty roasters who sell thirty-ton pallets of beans to hundreds of hopeful entrepreneurs."  

### Scene 12: The Final Executive Verdict
- **Visual**: The camera slowly zooms in on the Anurag Mascot standing in front of the café at night, holding his clipboard and addressing the audience directly with a calm, knowing smile.  
- **Voiceover**:  
  "If your dream is to sit in a beautiful sunlit room, drink specialty pour-overs, and curate indie playlists... do not open a café."  
  *[500ms pause]*  
  "Just buy a six-hundred-rupee bag of beans, brew it at home, and keep your one point five crores in a simple index fund."  
  *[400ms pause]*  
  "Because opening a café in Bangalore doesn't make you a founder. It makes you an unpaid manager for your landlord's commercial property."
