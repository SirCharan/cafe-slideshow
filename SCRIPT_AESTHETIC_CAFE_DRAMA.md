### 3. The 3 Deadly Killers of Aesthetic Cafe Unit Economics
1. **The 'Remote Worker' Dead Zone (11 AM - 5 PM):** Solo developers monopolize 65% of the 80 seats with a single ₹220 cold brew for 5 hours. Table turnover plummets from 4.0x to 0.8x. High-spending lunchtime groups walk away at the door.
2. **The Instagram Decay Curve:** Months 1–4 are packed with lifestyle creators and reels. By Month 6, footfall drops 35–40% when the next brutalist microcement rival opens 300 meters away.
3. **The CapEx Depreciation Treadmill:** Long before the 52-month breakeven arrives, customer wear-and-tear forces a ₹30-40 Lakh interior overhaul just to maintain relevance on social media.

## Production & Speech Synthesis Guidelines
1. **Pacing & Breathing:** Keep exact 1.5s - 2.5s gaps between voice lines to let financial callouts, 3D floorplan animations, and invoice chops settle on screen.
2. **Audio Mixing:**
   - `ref_expressive_1` (Dramatic): -16 LUFS, rich low-end presence, close-mic proximity, satirical edge.
   - `ref4` (Financial Math): -14 LUFS, clinical, punchy, crisp broadcast diction.
3. **Phonetic Pronunciations:**
   - Acronyms expanded: `B-E-S-C-O-M`, `U-P-I`, `P-O-S`, `C-O-G-S`, `F-and-B`.
   - Numbers expanded into clear Indian spoken denominations (`one point five crore`, `forty lakh fifty thousand rupees`, `fifty-two months`).
